How it works

What is Abra.bitoinyield?

A investment dashboard for BTC and ETH. You invest USDT through our contact support, add the fund to your balance after deposited.

How do Buy Up / Buy Down work?

Open the Contract page, pick a coin, a contract length and a stake. Buy Up wins if the price is higher when the contract ends, Buy Down wins if it is lower. A win pays +80% of your stake, a loss forfeits it, and a tie returns it.

How do I add my funds?

Go to Assets and tap Add funds, You can only add fund deposit with our support.

Can I deposit or withdraw real money?

Yes. You can deposit and withdraw by contacting our customer support and following the steps. Your funds will be sent to the wallet address saved on your profile.
