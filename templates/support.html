How it works

What is abra.bitcoinyield?

A investment dashboard for BTC and ETH. You invest USDT through our contact support add the funds to your balance after deposited.

How do Buy Up / Buy Down work?

Open the Contract page, pick a coin, a contract length and a stake. Buy Up wins if the price is higher when the contract ends, Buy Down wins if it is lower. A win pays +80% of your stake, a loss forfeits it, and a tie returns it.

How do I add my funds?

Go to Assets and tap Add deposited funds, or reset everything from your Profile page.

Why does my balance go up and down?

Funds you add as BTC or ETH are held as coins at the price when you added them. Their value follows the live market price, so it can rise or fall even if you don't trade. Cash (USDT) stays fixed and is what contracts use.
